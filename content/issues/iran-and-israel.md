I strongly oppose and unequivocally condemn the Israeli government's plan to
ethnically cleanse the Palestinians into other countries. Palestinians have
every right to live on their land with equal rights. The idea that people
whose ancestors lived in a land 2000 years ago have a right to it today,
while people whose ancestors lived there 100 years ago don't, is ludicrous.

I also oppose and condemn the rise of antisemitism in our country and
unequivocally affirm that criticizing the actions of the Israeli government
and demanding freedom and dignity for the Palestinians is not antisemitism.
Hating people because they are Jewish is wrong while hating the actions of
the Israeli government is necessary for any person with a moral compass.
